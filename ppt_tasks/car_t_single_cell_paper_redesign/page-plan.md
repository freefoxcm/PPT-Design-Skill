# Page plan — research editorial rebuild v2

| Page | Goal | Primary visual architecture | Main claim / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the paper question | restrained cover with atlas field + clinical-to-cellular arc | conceptual framing only |
| 2 | Define the clinical signal | large editable persistence timeline with 8.4-year anchor | observed clinical phenotype |
| 3 | Show how the study measures the signal | vertical sample-to-readout pipeline | cohort and assay facts |
| 4 | Make the atlas legible | enlarged UMAP map with editable family legend | 695,819 cells, 17 states |
| 5 | Build the clinical axis | ordered BCA strata strip with selected cohort facts | association axis, not binary outcome |
| 6 | Show type 2 convergence | one dominant Fig. 2 composition + enlarged readout callouts | transcriptome, secretion, chromatin converge |
| 7 | Separate mechanism from association | network-to-cluster-2 composition | candidate regulatory hub |
| 8 | Prove longitudinality | large serum time-series / heatmap composition | serial evidence, not snapshot |
| 9 | Show functional test | experiment timeline + two enlarged result panels | preclinical recall model |
| 10 | Show engineering direction | IL-4 priming workflow + dose comparison + result panel | preclinical manufacturing strategy |
| 11 | Summarize evidence levels | evidence ladder with figure refs | do not collapse evidence types |
| 12 | Close with contribution and limits | sparse takeaway + evidence map | bounded conclusion |

Design rule: each data page gets one focal figure, one sentence-level takeaway, and one explicit evidence boundary. Secondary evidence is enlarged selected panels or editable diagrams, not a wall of thumbnails.
