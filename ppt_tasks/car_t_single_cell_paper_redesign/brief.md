## Outcome

What must the audience understand, remember, feel, or do?

The audience should remember one bounded argument: durable B-cell aplasia is the clinical entry point; a type 2-like CAR T state is the cellular correlate; cluster 2 is a candidate regulatory hub; longitudinal serum and mouse studies test persistence and function; IL-4 is a preclinical engineering direction, not a clinical efficacy claim.
